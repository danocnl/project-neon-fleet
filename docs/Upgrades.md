### **1\. Stat Mutators (Generic & Ship-Tied)**

Bread-and-butter upgrades that alter hardware parameters. These establish baseline builds and scale cleanly with recursive mechanics.

* **Gating / Progression:** Available right from Tier 1 (Common).  
* **Downside Mechanics:** High-tier stat mutators use "Min-Max" trade-offs (e.g., *\+50% Shield Pool, but \-20% Top Velocity*).  
* **Ship Specificity:** Ships with unique hardware (like the **Krait's** drone bays or **Type-10's** turrets) get specialized stat nodes (e.g., *\+Drone Hull* or *\+Turret Tracking Speed*).

### **2\. Logic & Trigger Gates (Automation Core)**

Upgrades that unlock new **IF / THEN** automation behaviors and targeting vectors for your ship's AI routines.

* **Gating / Progression:** Gated behind specific Class Specialization picks or reaching Sector Benchmarks.  
* **Recursive Scaling:** Picking multiple logic modules allows conditional chaining (e.g., *IF Shield \< 20% THEN Trigger Phase Shift $\\rightarrow$ IF Phase Active THEN Overclock Lasers*).  
* **Co-Op Synergies:** Unlocks triggers based on teammate states (e.g., *IF Ally is targeted by \>3 enemies THEN redirect energy to point-defense*).

### **3\. Cross-Class Converters (Downside / High-Risk Modules)**

Game-changing modules that completely alter how a resource or stat functions, forcing a playstyle shift.

* **Gating / Progression:** Tier 3+ (Requires picking 2 prerequisite upgrades in a specific tag tree).  
* **Downside Mechanics:** Converts one resource into another entirely (e.g., *Converts 100% of Max Shield into Armor Plating; Shield capacity becomes 0*).  
* **Synergy Potential:** Allows unusual builds, like running a **Hyper-Conductor** specialization on a zero-shield hull by converting heat generation into direct energy pulses instead.

### **4\. Tag Resonance / Multipliers (Recursive Engine)**

Upgrades that don't grant direct flat stats, but instead amplify every other card you own that shares a specific tag (e.g., LASER, GRAVITY, DRONE).

* **Gating / Progression:** Gated until a player holds at least 3 cards with the matching tag.  
* **Recursive Mechanics:** Increases in strength exponentially as the run progresses (e.g., *For every DRONE tag in your build, all active constructs gain \+10% movement speed and \+5% splash radius*).

### **5\. Class Apex Keystone Upgrades (Unique Class Capstones)**

Exclusive, high-impact modules unique to each of the 9 Class Specializations.

* **Gating / Progression:** Gated behind Tier 4/5 (Requires deep investment in the class's signature tag tree).  
* **Co-Op Synergies:** Acts as the team's ultimate power spikes (e.g., **Chrono Architect Keystone:** *Time bubbles now affect the entire arena map simultaneously for 5 seconds upon taking fatal damage*).
